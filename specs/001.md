Claiming settled puts zeroes collateral by converting the wrong variable
claimSettled uses the exercised balance to compute cash payout for put netting and ignores the unexercised collateral tracked in settledDepositToken, causing collateral freed by netting to become unclaimable for cash-secured puts.

Affected Locations

src/OPair.sol
L522-535
\_settleNetted credits settledDepositToken with unexercised short size and settledSwapToken with exercised size when a short is netted by a new long before expiry.

src/OPair.sol
L439-454
claimSettled computes depAmt from swapAmt when isCall is false, discarding the credited settledDepositToken amount and only converting the exercised portion, so unexercised collateral for cash-secured puts is never returned.

Impact
Any put seller who nets their short position via a buy before expiry loses the posted cash collateral: settledDepositToken is cleared but depAmt is overwritten with \_cashAmount(swapAmt), so if no exercised portion exists swapAmt is zero and the function transfers nothing. The vault retains the freed cash, totalSold is reduced to zero, and the seller has no remaining short position to claim against, permanently stranding their funds and shrinking the system’s backing.

Proof of concept
Create a put OPair and have Alice sell 1 option (posts strike-denominated cash). 2) Before any exercise occurs, Alice buys 1 option to flatten: \_settleNetted sets settledDepositToken[Alice]=1, settledSwapToken[Alice]=0 and reduces totalSold. 3) Alice calls claimSettled. In the isCall==false branch depAmt is set to \_cashAmount(swapAmt)=0 and swapAmt remains 0, so no tokens are transferred. Alice’s collateral stays trapped in the vault with no remaining position to claim, resulting in a direct loss.
Remediation
In claimSettled, compute depAmt from settledDepositToken for put pairs: e.g., cache the original depAmt before zeroing and set depAmt = \_cashAmount(depAmt) when !isCall, leaving swapAmt unchanged. Do not overwrite depAmt with swapAmt.
